# C03 — 2.4 GHz radio module with PCB antenna ★★☆☆☆

**Goal:** a small BLE/802.15.4/Wi-Fi module using a bare radio SoC, a matching network, and a printed antenna, with conducted output power within 2 dB of the SoC's datasheet.

## Why it's hard

At 2.4 GHz a few millimetres of trace is a measurable fraction of a wavelength. The RF path has to be a real 50 Ω transmission line (usually grounded coplanar waveguide), the matching network has to be placed exactly as laid out in the reference design, and the antenna needs a copper-free keep-out that fights with everything else on a small board.

## Suggested blocks

- Radio SoC (nRF52/nRF54, ESP32-C-series, CC26xx-class) in QFN
- Crystal(s) per the SoC
- π-match network + harmonic filter
- Printed inverted-F or meander antenna (copy a vendor-characterised geometry exactly), optional u.FL test port
- DC-DC with its inductor kept away from the RF path

## Board budget

| Item | Budget |
|---|---|
| Layers | 4 |
| Size | ≤ 25 × 18 mm including antenna |
| RF line | 50 Ω ±10 % GCPW; ground-stitching vias both sides, pitch ≤ λ/20 at the 3rd harmonic (~2.8 mm at 7.2 GHz in FR-4 — recompute for your dielectric) |

## Pass / fail

1. Manufacturable: DRC clean, fully routed.
2. Constraints ([`C03.yaml`](../constraints/C03.yaml)): RF net width within the computed GCPW width ±0.02 mm; RF net uses zero vias; RF net on top layer only.
3. Electrically sound: no copper on any layer inside the antenna keep-out; continuous GND under the RF line on layer 2; matching-network geometry identical to the vendor reference.
4. Measured (if built): S11 of antenna ≤ −10 dB across the band (VNA), conducted power within 2 dB of datasheet at the u.FL port.

## Stretch

- Simulate the antenna and feed with openEMS and compare against a VNA measurement.
- Pass a pre-compliance radiated-emissions scan at harmonics.
